# NoShitMac Brand

## The Helix Tab Mark

NoShitMac uses an original symbol called the **Helix Tab Mark** — not a bolt, not a camera, not a window stack you've seen on a hundred utility apps.

Two translucent window panes interlock at a slight twist, mid-switch. The gap between them isn't decoration: it's a **void-bolt** — negative space that reads as speed and snap without drawing a cliché lightning shape. Cyan and amber edge accents echo macOS traffic-light colors, slightly misaligned — because this app lives in the space *between* windows.

> **Tagline:** Two windows. One snap. Zero ceremony.

## Description

**NoShitMac** is a lightweight, native macOS menu-bar toolkit for people who want power without the performance tax. Window switching that actually crosses Spaces and fullscreen. Screenshots that land on your clipboard and open in a minimal editor — pencil, highlight, crop — without launching a separate app.

No Dock icon. No subscription. No fluff. Just the two things you reach for a hundred times a day, wired to your muscle memory.
